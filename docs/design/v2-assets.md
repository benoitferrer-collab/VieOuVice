## Prompt utilisé

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background illustration for VieGame mobile turn-based battle UI, no text or UI. Create a polished original 3D cartoon dark fantasy arena, portrait 3:4 composition. Deep midnight navy sky, luminous lavender full moon high in center, distant ruined gothic stone arches and tall angular basalt pillars, violet mist at the bottom. Two empty floating circular rocky battle platforms: left foreground at 70 percent down, right middle distance at 52 percent down. Platforms broad flat tops, suitable for compositing a character onto each later. They must be empty. Soft purple rim lighting, small scattered lime fireflies, tactile stylized stone, charming mysterious friendly game atmosphere, premium console-game aesthetic. Keep central action space spacious and contrast low enough to overlay characters and health UI. Full bleed, no border. No characters, no creatures, no reapers, no weapons, no interface, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
